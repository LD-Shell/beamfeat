    Citing beamfeat

    If you use beamfeat in published work, please cite:

    1. The paper, which describes the method. Always cite it.
    2. The software release, when your results depend on a particular version.
    3. The statistical methods behind the options you used, which beamfeat implements but did not invent.

    The paper

    Daramola, O. M. (2026). Symbolic feature construction with false discovery rate control. Transactions on Machine Learning Research. https://openreview.net/forum?id=UibXoy4h3u

    @article{daramola2026symbolic,
  title   = {Symbolic feature construction with false discovery rate control},
  author  = {Daramola, Olanrewaju M.},
  journal = {Transactions on Machine Learning Research},
  issn    = {2835-8856},
  year    = {2026},
  url     = {https://openreview.net/forum?id=UibXoy4h3u}
}

    The paper describes version 0.4.2. Later releases correct two of its results: a harness bug understated featuretools, and fixing a search bug lowers the number of datasets where beamfeat leads both tree ensembles from five to four. Reading the paper today has the details.

    The software release

    Each release is archived on Zenodo with its own DOI:

    what to cite DOI
    beamfeat in general (resolves to the latest release) 10.5281/zenodo.21619567
    0.4.2, the version the paper describes, with the run records behind its results 10.5281/zenodo.23417132

    To cite the release you used, open the first DOI, choose that version from the list of versions on the Zenodo page, and use its DOI. The page also gives the citation in several formats.

    @software{daramola_beamfeat,
  author    = {Daramola, Olanrewaju M.},
  title     = {beamfeat: beam-search feature construction with FDR-controlled selection},
  publisher = {Zenodo},
  doi       = {10.5281/zenodo.21619567},
  url       = {https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21619567}
}

    For one release, add version = {...} and replace the DOI with that release's. CITATION.cff in the repository has the same details in machine-readable form, and GitHub's "Cite this repository" button reads it.

    The statistical methods

    beamfeat's guarantee rests on established procedures. If your work relies on one of them, please cite its source as well:

    if you use beamfeat relies on please also cite
    any fit with the default selector permutation p-values with the add-one estimator, and the Benjamini–Yekutieli correction Phipson & Smyth (2010); Benjamini & Yekutieli (2001)
    selection_correction="bh" the Benjamini–Hochberg correction, in place of Benjamini–Yekutieli Benjamini & Hochberg (1995)
    selector="knockoff" or KnockoffSelector fixed-X knockoffs with knockoff+ when there are more than twice as many rows as columns, model-X Gaussian knockoffs otherwise Barber & Candès (2015); Candès et al. (2018)
    KnockoffSelector(s_method="ci") the conditional-independence knockoff Liu & Rigollet (2019)
    parsimony_alpha the multiplier bootstrap for the best fit noise can reach Fan & Zhou (2016)
    input_test=True sequential Monte Carlo p-values Besag & Clifford (1991)
    parsimony_holdout with certify_test="conditional" the Freedman–Lane permutation test Freedman & Lane (1983); Anderson & Robinson (2001); Winkler et al. (2014)
    screen_equation permutation p-values with the add-one estimator and the Benjamini–Yekutieli correction, or Benjamini–Hochberg; with input_test=True, sequential Monte Carlo p-values Phipson & Smyth (2010); Benjamini & Yekutieli (2001) or Benjamini & Hochberg (1995); Besag & Clifford (1991)

    Statistical guarantees explains what each procedure guarantees in beamfeat, and under which assumptions.
